# Migration data

Inputs for the once-off migration. They live **outside the repo and the plugin**, in `../migration-data/` beside the repo (`C:\dev\ink\migration-data` on Windows). `INK_MIGRATION_DATA_DIR` in the site's `wp-config.php` names that folder (`tools/rehearsal-prep.mjs` sets it), and `Ink\Kernel\MigrationData` reads from it. There is no fallback: `wp ink migrate-all` and `rehearsal-prep.mjs` both stop before doing anything if a file is missing.

- `writer-accounts.csv`: `name,user_login`. A by-line as printed on the old result posts (pen names, maiden names, spelling variants) and the account it belongs to. Hand-verified in the July 2026 cleanup. Used by `wp ink derive-tiers`. Some logins are email addresses.
- `writer-aliases.csv`: `variant,canonical`. Misspellings and variants of one by-line, mapped to its canonical form (the canonical form is then looked up in `writer-accounts.csv` or among the site's users).
- `result-post-authors.csv`: `login`. The moderator accounts that publish the challenge result posts ("... Kommentaar en uitslae"). Their posts are kept as winners announcements instead of being filed as submitted writing, and are read for gradering. Users who can `edit_others_posts` also count, but `Inkmoderator` is only a subscriber after the user-reclassification step, so the list is what matters.

Add a row to `writer-accounts.csv` or `writer-aliases.csv` when `derive-tiers` reports an unresolved writer that you identify.

**Winner lists (hand-curated; nothing reads them yet).** They are the source of truth, meant to replace parsing during migration as the single source for grades, winner marks and lifetime win counts. `docs/migration-tools/build-winner-lists.php` only adds to them: it appends wins from result posts not yet listed and fills a blank post or account. It never rebuilds them and never changes what a row already says, so running it is always safe. Decisions are made by editing the files. The previous lists are kept in `backup/`.

- `wenners.csv`: one row per win (all categories), with round, grade, position, by-line, account, matched post and where it came from. A win counts even with no post (owner, 2026-10-31). Status: `ok`; `review` for a person to check; `best-effort`, which nobody checks. All count as wins. **A member who has posted nothing in a year is best effort** (owner, 2026-10-31): if the winning post can be identified it is marked; if not, we move on.
- `result-posts.csv`: each moderator result post and its round. Status also has `not-results` (apologies for late results) and `outside` (competitions, not counted).
- `challenges.csv`: each round, with its entries, result posts and wins. Competitions are not challenges (owner), so they are left out. Februarie 2019 and Herfs published no results (owner).
- `source_category` in these lists is the round's prod category term, which identifies a round. `challenge_id` changes with every migration and is refreshed from it.
- `writer-deleted.csv`: `name,note`. By-lines whose account is gone: the win is recorded and promotes nobody. Partly the owner's best guess. The `anonymous` account (#1853) earns no wins; Charne Harding's account was deleted and her posts moved there.
- `review/accounts-confirmed-*.csv`: the owner's y/n answers to `accounts-to-confirm.csv`; those are not asked again.
- `review/wins-without-a-post.csv`, `review/accounts-to-confirm.csv` (no post of its own, but another account has the same title in the round's dates), `review/july-list-mismatches.csv` (result posts before July 2026 only), `review/grade-changes.csv`: rewritten each run, active writers only or sorted first.

Comparing text: people can't type Afrikaans reliably, so accents get dropped or swapped and the article 'n arrives as ŉ, ñ, ń, 'n, ’n, n or N. Writers also add their grade, class, word count and project to titles ("(Silwer gradering)", "[brons]", "Goudklas", "Katergorie - Goud"); a grade word that belongs to the title stays ("Drie silwer bangles"). `Ink\Migration\TextKey` folds all of that away; compare titles and names only through it.

Matching rules: a result comes after its round closes and at most about 3 months later (owner), so an entry is looked for in the 150 days before the result post. Winning posts are filed under their round, which confirms both the post and the round. A writer's post may add to the title ("Die onderhoud (Geskiedenisfiksie)"), or hold it ("Villanelle: Na jou asem weg is"). If nothing fits the window, the writer's own exact title still identifies the post, whatever its date or status (some were reposted later), as does an attached image with that title. The same title by another account is only noted, never assigned. The by-line "Francois Meyer" covers two writers: `FrancoisMeyer` (#46, 13 posts up to 2019-09-10) and `Francois`/GEDAGTES (#66), so the matched post decides. Palet and Suzette Strydom are one person: use `Palet` (#168); merging #69 and its 3 posts is not built.

- `kompetisies-oud.csv` (**optional overrides only**; the file may be missing; `lovable-delta-2026-10/specs/05-kompetisies.md` §3.6): neither the galas, their photos nor the 2022 competition are in it. `wp ink migrate-kompetisies` finds them in the old page tree, **by meta first**: the owner runs `docs/migration-tools/tag-old-competitions.sql` on production before the snapshot, which tags each gala Page (`ink_old_gala` = year), its Fotos Page (`ink_old_gala_fotos` = year) and the 2022 competition Page 49428 (`ink_old_competition` = 2022). Untagged, a gala is still found by its title (a top-level Page titled "...Gala..." with a child Page titled "Foto...") and the step warns "found by title; tag it"; an untagged competition is not found. Name, year and body come from the gala or competition Page, photos (3,379 for the ten galas, in page order) from the shortcodes of the Fotos Page. A gala added to production before the cutover imports in full: tag it, or let the title fallback find it. Columns, all but `sleutel` optional: `sleutel` (`gala-<jaar>`, `kompetisie-2022`, or a bare year), `hoogtepunt` (**owner**), `onderskrifte` (**owner**: one `attachment-id|caption` per line, the id as in the Fotos Page's shortcodes or the media library; written to the attachment's caption), and `naam`, `jaar`, `bladsy`, `fotos_bladsy` (override; only needed to describe a competition no tag marks). A blank cell overrides nothing. Old competitions only; new ones are added on the site. Blank cells import and are listed; they never stop `migrate-all`. Backup of the old 151 KB version with every photo path: `backup/kompetisies-oud.csv`.
- `inkpols-uitgawes.csv`: one row per InkPols uitgawe, owner-maintained. `slug` and `naam` (the months, e.g. `Julie/Augustus 2026`, shown as the eyebrow), `titel` and `beskrywing` (filled in by hand), `datum` (the first month of the issue, used as the publish date, so the newest row is the current issue), `pdf` and `voorblad` (paths under `wp-content/uploads/`, matching the prod attachments; the cover is also the social image), `ou_url` (the old prod Page, redirected to the new URL), and the three featured items `glans_1`, `glans_2`, `glans_3` (owner ruling 2026-11-03: a variety of items from the magazine, no longer one Gedig, one Storie and one Artikel), each with three columns: `glans_N_titel`, `glans_N_skrywer` and `glans_N_tipe` (free text saying what the item is, e.g. "Kortverhaal", shown as its label; max 40 characters). They are plain text describing something in the PDF, shown in glans order. All nine are required. On prod, the February issue's PDF was uploaded twice; the file listed is the one its page used. Read by `wp ink migrate-inkpols`: a row with any blank required cell (everything except `ou_url`) becomes a draft issue, and the step lists the missing cells.
